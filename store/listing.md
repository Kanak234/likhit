# Microsoft Store listing — Likhit
**Category:** Productivity (secondary: Education)
**Short description:** Turn photos of handwritten notes into an editable Word document — offline or with AI.
**Description:** Likhit converts photos or scanned PDFs of handwritten pages into a real Word (.docx) file with headings, bullet lists and highlighted unclear words. It picks the best reader available: your own AI API key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini), a local vision model on your PC (Ollama / LM Studio), or the built-in offline OCR (Tesseract, included). In local and offline modes nothing leaves your computer.
**Features**
- Photos (JPG/PNG) and PDFs → editable .docx
- Three modes: Cloud AI · Local LLM · Offline OCR (bundled)
- Real Word headings, bullets, page breaks, original photo under each page
- Unclear words highlighted in yellow for quick review
- Works on Windows and Linux (Snap Store)
**Keywords:** handwriting to Word, OCR, notes, docx, students, offline
